# How to Contribute

**Welcome.** Especially if you're skeptical.

This started as one person's attempt to name a pattern: brilliant people keep building systems that replace them, and their work enriches others.

But what comes next? **That's not for one person to decide.**

---

## This Is an Invitation to Build the Vision

We don't have a blueprint. We have a starting point and a lot of questions.

**Your contribution is helping us figure out what this should actually be.**

---

## Questions We're Asking

### **About the Vision**

- What vision of the world will help you preserve people and things that you hold dear?
- How can people be free through collectively owned intelligence?
- What problems can shared infrastructure solve?
- What is your meaning of "fairest"?
- How would we measure if this is working?

### **About What We're Building**

- What are the least number of things that we need to build really well towards a future where our beloved people and beings can thrive?
- What resources do people actually have to contribute?
- What all can people contribute?
- What do people need in order to build a thriving future?
- How can we turn this into something like sunlight — abundant and available to anyone?
- What mistakes of the past do we want to learn from and build better?

### **About How People Participate**

- How can we ensure that people can give something of value? (Sometimes it takes an hour, sometimes 10 seconds)
- How do we make this accessible to people who can't code?
- How do we acknowledge contributions fairly?
- What stops people from participating and how do we remove those barriers?
- How do different skills and perspectives combine?

### **About Governance**

- How do we make decisions without recreating power concentration?
- What happens when people disagree?
- How do we prevent bad actors from hijacking this?
- What does "owned by everyone, controlled by no one" actually mean?
- Should there be any structure at all, or should it emerge naturally?

### **About Sustainability**

- How much energy does this really need?
- Can this scale without becoming extractive itself?
- How do we fund this without compromising independence?
- What makes this last beyond the initial enthusiasm?

### **About Impact**

- How does this change people's lives?
- What opportunities does this create?
- Who might be harmed and how do we prevent that?
- What does success look like?

### **What Are the Other Questions We Need to Ask?**

What are we missing? What should we be asking that we're not?

---

## How to Contribute

**Answer a question.** Pick one that interests you and share your thoughts in an issue.

**Ask a better question.** If we're missing something important, tell us.

**Challenge an assumption.** Point out where we're wrong or thinking too narrowly.

**Share an example.** Tell us about projects that tried something similar — what worked, what didn't.

**Propose something wild.** The best ideas often sound impossible at first.

**Connect dots.** See a pattern we're missing? Draw it out.

---

## What We Need

Honestly? **We don't know yet.**

We need people who can:
- Think clearly about complex systems
- Imagine possibilities beyond what exists
- Spot problems before they happen
- Build things (code, documents, communities, frameworks)
- Question everything (including this document)

If you're reading this and thinking "but I don't know how to..." — that's fine. **Nobody knows how to do this yet because it hasn't been done.**

---

## Current Focus

Right now, we're asking: **What should this actually be?**

Not the technology stack. Not the implementation details.

**What is the thing we're trying to create, and why does it matter?**

Once we have clarity on that, everything else follows.

---

## How to Start

1. **Read the [Manifesto](MANIFESTO.md)** — understand why this exists
2. **Pick a question** that resonates with you
3. **Open an issue** and share your thoughts
4. **Join the conversation** — build on what others are saying

---

**You don't need permission. You don't need expertise. You just need to care about the questions.**
