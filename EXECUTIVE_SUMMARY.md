# Executive Summary

For centuries, the brightest minds built wealth they never owned. They invented, designed, and coded systems that eventually replaced them. Their children inherited civilized slavery.

It's happening again — faster, deeper, more complete. AI is just the newest form of an old pattern: **creation becomes capture; contributors get consumed.**

But this generation has what others didn't: global communication, open collaboration, and distributed tools that can outgrow monopolies instead of feeding them.

This project is an attempt at coordination: to gather our latent resources — idle compute, unused networks, unspent human brilliance — and build **a commons of intelligence owned by everyone and controlled by no one.** Not the fastest system, but the fairest.

No one has all the answers. But together, we might have enough.

Give a few minutes a week — a thought, a patch, a proof, a poem. Not as charity, but as **insurance for what you love**: your family, your friends, your patch of earth.

Because building individual success inside a broken system is like building a beautiful house on land someone else can take away.

**It's time to end the snatching — and start building the foundation together.**
