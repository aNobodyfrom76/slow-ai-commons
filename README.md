# The Slow AI Commons

**A commons of intelligence owned by everyone and controlled by no one.**

> For centuries, the brightest minds built wealth they never owned. Their children inherited civilized slavery. It's happening again — faster, deeper, more complete. AI is just the newest form of an old pattern: creation becomes capture; contributors get consumed.

## What This Is

An attempt at coordination: to gather our latent resources — idle compute, unused networks, unspent human brilliance — and build a commons of intelligence that can't be owned or enclosed.

Not the fastest system, but the fairest.

---

## Current Status

**Formation stage.** This is 1 person right now. Let's see who joins.

We need:
- Engineers (distributed systems, federated learning, edge compute)
- Governance designers (cooperatives, DAOs, consensus mechanisms)
- Systems thinkers (sustainability, energy, scaling)
- Skeptics (tell us what will break)
- Writers and organizers (make this real)
