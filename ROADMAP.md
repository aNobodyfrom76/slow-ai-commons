# Roadmap

**This is a living document.** It will change as we learn.

---

## Current Status: Formation

**We are 1 person right now.**

The manifesto exists. The invitation is extended.

Now we need people to help figure out what comes next.

---

## Phase 1: Crystallize the Vision

**Goal:** Loosely crystallize the vision of the world we want to see.

Not a rigid plan, but a shared picture clear enough to align around.

**Key Questions:**
- What does a world with collectively-owned intelligence look like?
- What becomes possible that isn't possible now?
- Who thrives in this future?
- What does "fairest" actually mean in practice?
- How do we know we're moving toward this vision?

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Vision document (co-created, not dictated)
- [ ] Scenarios of what this enables
- [ ] Principles that emerge from the vision

**Timeline:** As long as it takes to feel true.

---

## Phase 2: Establish Universal Principles

**Goal:** Define principles we can agree on — no matter our race, religion, or background — that will make this work of undeniable value.

These principles will be the foundation that prevents capture and guides all future decisions.

**Key Questions:**
- What principles transcend individual interests?
- What makes this undeniably valuable to humanity?
- What would make us walk away from this project?
- What must never be compromised?
- How do we protect these principles from erosion?

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Core principles document
- [ ] Tests for whether decisions align with principles
- [ ] Red lines we won't cross

**Timeline:** Before any code is written or governance established.

---

## Phase 3: Design Governance

**Goal:** Create structures for decision-making that embody our principles.

**Key Questions:**
- How do we make decisions without recreating power concentration?
- How do we acknowledge contributors fairly?
- What prevents capture or corruption?
- How do we handle disagreements and forks?
- What's the legal structure, if any?

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Decision-making framework
- [ ] Contribution acknowledgment system
- [ ] Conflict resolution process
- [ ] Anti-capture mechanisms

**Timeline:** After principles are established.

---

## Phase 4: Define Technical Architecture

**Goal:** Agree on what we're actually building.

**Key Questions:**
- What's the minimum viable version?
- What can be shared without creating new dependencies?
- How do devices coordinate?
- What tasks can actually be distributed effectively?
- What's the energy cost?
- What has been tried before and what can we learn?

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Technical specification
- [ ] Architecture proposals (multiple welcome)
- [ ] Security model
- [ ] Energy efficiency analysis
- [ ] MVP definition

**Timeline:** After governance is clear.

---

## Phase 5: Build Proof of Concept

**Goal:** Demonstrate that the basic idea works.

**What it might look like:**
- Small distributed computation task
- 10-100 volunteer devices
- Basic coordination protocol
- Simple contribution tracking

**Not trying to:**
- Replace existing systems
- Scale prematurely
- Be production-ready

**Timeline:** 6-12 months after Phase 4 (guess).

## Anti-Roadmap: What We're Not Doing

- **No venture capital** (at least not in ways that create misaligned incentives)
- **No rushing to market** (this is infrastructure for the long term)
- **No single point of control** (even this roadmap can be forked)
- **No sacrificing principles for speed** (we get the foundation right first)
